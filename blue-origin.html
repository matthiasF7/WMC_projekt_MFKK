Blue Origin

History

Blue Origin was founded in 2000, and in 2006 it conducted its first test flights with early prototypes like Goddard. In 2015, the company achieved the first vertical landing of its reusable New Shepard rocket after a suborbital flight, and in 2016 it completed the first full reuse of the same booster. In 2021, Blue Origin flew its first crewed mission with New Shepard, carrying Jeff Bezos and other passengers to the edge of space. The company is now developing its New Glenn orbital rocket, aiming for its first flight in the second half of the 2020s.
