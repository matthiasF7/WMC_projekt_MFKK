United Launch Alliance

History

United Space Alliance was founded in 1996 as a joint venture between Boeing and Lockheed Martin to manage NASA's Space Shuttle program. The company handled shuttle operations, training, and maintenance until the Shuttle's retirement in 2011. After completing its final contracts, United Space Alliance closed in 2014. It then dissolved in 2019.
