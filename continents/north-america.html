North America

North America has been a pioneer in space exploration, with the United States leading numerous groundbreaking missions. The continent hosts several major space agencies and private companies that contribute significantly to global space endeavors.

Space Agencies & Companies in North America

North America is home to world-leading space organizations. The United States dominates the commercial space industry and leads in space exploration technology, while Canada contributes significantly to space research and robotics.

Major Organizations

SpaceX

Location: Hawthorne, California, USA

SpaceX revolutionized the space industry with reusable rockets. The company operates the Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy rockets, provides crew and cargo services to the ISS, and is developing Starship for lunar and Mars missions.

Blue Origin

Location: Kent, Washington, USA

Blue Origin develops reusable spacecraft and launch vehicles. New Shepard offers suborbital space tourism experiences, while New Glenn will provide heavy-lift orbital capabilities. The company is also developing lunar landers for NASA's Artemis program.

Key Facilities

  • Kennedy Space Center: Historic launch site in Florida for major missions
  • SpaceX Starbase: Texas facility for Starship development and testing
  • Blue Origin New Glenn Launch Site: Under development in Florida
  • Canadian Space Agency: Supports ISS operations and Earth observation missions
