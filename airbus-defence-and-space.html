Airbus Defence and Space

History

Airbus Defence and Space was officially founded in January 2014 as part of a major restructuring of the European Aeronautic Defence and Space Company (EADS). It brought together Airbus Military, Astrium, and Cassidian into one division, consolidating expertise in aerospace, defence, and space systems. Since then, the company has achieved milestones such as developing advanced military aircraft like the A400M transport plane, contributing to European space programs with satellites and launch vehicles, and expanding into cybersecurity and intelligence services. Today, Airbus Defence and Space plays a key role in global aerospace innovation, with headquarters in Taufkirchen, Germany, and operations worldwide.
