United Launch Alliance

History

United Launch Alliance (ULA) was established on December 1 2006, as a joint venture between Lockheed Martin and Boeing, merging their Atlas and Delta rocket programs. ULA logged its 36th successful mission in 36 months by December 2009, demonstrating early consistent launch reliability. In October 2015 the company achieved its 100th consecutive successful launch, maintaining a flawless launch record up to that point. On June 5 2024 ULA launched its first crewed mission, carrying NASA astronauts aboard the CST-100 Starliner spacecraft atop an Atlas V.
