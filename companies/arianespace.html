Arianespace

Arianespace SA

Arianespace is a European space launch service provider founded in 1980. Headquartered in Évry, France, Arianespace provides launch services for commercial, civil, and institutional payloads using the Ariane family of launch vehicles.

Overview

Arianespace operates from launch facilities in French Guiana and operates the Ariane 5 vehicle, which has one of the highest mission success rates in the industry. The company is transitioning to the Ariane 6 for next-generation capabilities.

Launch Vehicles

  • Ariane 5: Heavy-lift launcher with unmatched reliability and dual-payload capability
  • Ariane 6: Next-generation launcher with increased performance and cost efficiency
  • Vega: Small-lift vehicle for medium-sized satellite deployment

Mission Heritage

Arianespace has established an impressive track record with over 300 successful launches. The company serves the telecommunications, Earth observation, and scientific communities worldwide.

European Commitment

Arianespace represents European launch independence and is critical for Europe's access to space. The company is investing in the future with Ariane 6 and exploring innovative launch solutions including reusable rocket technology.
