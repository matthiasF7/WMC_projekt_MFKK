Arianespace

History

Arianespace was founded in 1980 as the world's first commercial launch services provider, created to operate the Ariane family of rockets. In 1984, it achieved its first major success with the Ariane 3, which boosted Europe's presence in the commercial satellite market. Throughout the 1990s and 2000s, Ariane 4 and Ariane 5 became highly reliable workhorses, securing Arianespace's position as a global leader in commercial launches. In 2023, the Ariane 5 completed its final flight, and Arianespace is now transitioning to the next-generation Ariane 6, which aims to continue Europe's independent access to space.
