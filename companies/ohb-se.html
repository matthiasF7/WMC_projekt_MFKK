OHB SE

OHB SE is a European space and technology company headquartered in Bremen, Germany. The company develops and builds satellites, space systems, and technological solutions for both institutional and commercial customers.

Overview

Founded in 1981, OHB has grown into one of Europe's leading space companies. The company works closely with the European Space Agency (ESA), national governments, and commercial partners. OHB focuses on satellite development, space exploration technologies, and security-related space systems.

Key Programs

  • Galileo Satellites: OHB builds navigation satellites for the European Galileo navigation system.
  • Earth Observation Satellites: Development of satellites used for environmental monitoring, mapping, and climate research.
  • Exploration Systems: Participation in European exploration missions and scientific space projects.

Mission Portfolio

OHB has contributed to several major European space projects, including navigation, Earth observation, and space science missions. The company provides complete satellite systems as well as individual components and payload technologies.

Future Direction

OHB continues to expand its role in European space programs, focusing on next-generation satellite technologies, exploration missions, and commercial space applications. The company also supports security and defense-related space infrastructure projects.
