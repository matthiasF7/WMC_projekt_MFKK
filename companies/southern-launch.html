Southern Launch

Southern Launch is an Australian commercial space company specializing in launch services and launch infrastructure. The company was founded in 2017 and plays a key role in establishing Australia as a space launch nation.

Overview

Southern Launch provides safe, regulated launch sites for suborbital and orbital missions. The company works with domestic and international space organizations, startups, and research institutions to support rocket testing and spaceflight operations.

Key Capabilities

  • Operation of licensed launch facilities in South Australia
  • Support for suborbital and orbital rocket launches
  • Launch logistics, safety, and regulatory compliance
  • Collaboration with commercial and research partners

Launch Sites

Southern Launch operates the Koonibba Test Range and the Whalers Way Orbital Launch Complex. These sites are designed to support a wide range of launch vehicles and mission profiles, helping Australia enter the global launch market.

Role in Australian Space Industry

By providing modern launch infrastructure, Southern Launch enables innovation within Australia's space sector and supports the development of sovereign launch capabilities. The company contributes to economic growth and high-technology job creation.
