LandSpace Technology Corporation

LandSpace Technology Corporation

LandSpace Technology Corporation is a Chinese private aerospace company headquartered in Beijing. Founded in 2015, the company focuses on the development of commercial launch vehicles and advanced propulsion technologies.

Overview

LandSpace is one of China's leading commercial space startups and aims to provide reliable and cost-effective launch services. The company is particularly known for its focus on liquid-fueled and methane-powered rocket engines, aligning with modern trends in reusable and cleaner propulsion systems.

Launch Vehicles

  • ZhuQue-1 (ZQ-1): Small orbital launch vehicle designed for lightweight payloads
  • ZhuQue-2 (ZQ-2): Medium-lift launch vehicle powered by liquid oxygen-methane engines

Mission Portfolio

LandSpace focuses primarily on commercial satellite launches, including payloads for Earth observation, communication, and technology demonstration missions. The company represents an important step in the commercialization of China's space sector.

Future Direction

LandSpace is working on reusable rocket technology and further development of methane-fueled engines. The company aims to expand its launch cadence and compete internationally in the growing commercial space market.
