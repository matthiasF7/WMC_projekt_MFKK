# **Website Project Description**

## **Spaceflight companies**

## **1. Wep Apllication**

New page where you can see satelittes around a globe.

## **2. Project Idea**

Website with information about Spaceflight companies, their history and the future of them.

---

## **3. Target Audience**

The Website is for people who want to learn more about these types of companies and want to see what is near to them.

---

## **4. Structure of the Website**

It will have a mainpage from where you can go to other websites with the companies, the history, ongoing projects and more.

---

## **5. Design and Layout**

We will use the main colors of the companies.
There will be sections with text and pictures.
There will also be a footer that's the same on all pages.

---

## **6. Milestones**

  * **Week 1:** Planning and creating the structure and researching about spaceflight companies.
  * **Week 2:** Building HTML pages and doing more research.
  * **Week 3:** Styling with CSS
  * **Week 4:** Testing and final presentation
