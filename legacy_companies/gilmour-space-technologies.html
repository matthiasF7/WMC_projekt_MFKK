Gilmour Space Technologies

Gilmour Space Technologies is an Australian private space company focused on developing launch vehicles and space systems. It was founded in 2015 by brothers Adam and James Gilmour and is based in Queensland, Australia.

Overview

The company aims to provide affordable and reliable access to space for small satellites. Gilmour Space Technologies designs, manufactures, and tests its own rockets, propulsion systems, and satellite platforms.

Key Technologies

  • Hybrid rocket propulsion systems
  • Eris: orbital small-lift launch vehicle
  • G-Sat: satellite platform solutions
  • In-house rocket and engine manufacturing

Current Projects

Gilmour Space Technologies is currently developing the Eris launch vehicle, designed to deploy small satellites into low Earth orbit. The company is also expanding its satellite manufacturing and space systems capabilities.

Role in the Australian Space Industry

As one of Australia's leading launch startups, Gilmour Space Technologies plays an important role in establishing sovereign launch capability and strengthening Australia's presence in the global space sector.
